LLMs can’t jump

The claim is not that models reason badly. It is that one of the three inferential modes is structurally absent, and it happens to be the one that produces new premises rather than new conclusions.
I.
Induction
Statistical pattern matching. Compression of what has already been observed.
Mastered
II.
Deduction
Formal proof from established premises. Theorem follows from axiom.
Being conquered
III.
Abduction
The jump. Generating the axioms themselves when the data is too sparse to compress.
Absent
Einstein as the test case: general relativity was reached from scarce observation, so creativity-as-compression cannot account for it.
Tom Zahavy, Google DeepMind · 27 Jan 2026 · philsci-archive.pitt.edu/28024
